## Caption — English

Most dashboards are opened once and never again. They are built for the person who made them, not the person who has to decide something.

A dashboard earns its place when a manager can open it on their phone, in a corridor, before a meeting, and know within ten seconds whether things are fine.

Colour-coded KPIs. The gap between target and actual. Where the problem is, and whose desk it sits on. Nothing else.

That is what we build.
