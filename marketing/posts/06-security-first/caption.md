## Caption — English

Before you hand your operational data to anyone, ask three questions:

1. Where is it stored, and who can reach it?
2. Who can see what — is access controlled by role?
3. What happens to it if we stop working together?

If they cannot answer clearly, that is your answer.

Every system we build has encrypted credentials, role-based access, validated inputs and hardened endpoints — not bolted on at the end, but from the first line of code.

## Caption — Kiswahili

Kabla ya kumkabidhi mtu yeyote takwimu za kazi zako, uliza maswali matatu:

1. Zinahifadhiwa wapi, na nani anaweza kuzifikia?
2. Nani anaona nini — je, ufikiaji unadhibitiwa kwa nafasi ya mtu?
3. Zitakuwaje tukiacha kufanya kazi pamoja?

Kama hawezi kujibu kwa uwazi, hilo ndilo jibu lako.

Kila mfumo tunaoujenga una siri zilizosimbwa, ufikiaji kwa nafasi, uthibitishaji wa taarifa zinazoingia, na milango iliyoimarishwa — si nyongeza ya mwisho, bali tangu mstari wa kwanza wa msimbo.
