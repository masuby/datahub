## Caption — English

If you lend money, you already know which report matters: portfolio at risk, by branch, by officer, today — not last month.

Most institutions still assemble it by hand. By the time it is ready, it describes a problem you can no longer prevent.

We build lending dashboards that show PAR, disbursements, collections and officer performance live, and that push follow-up lists straight to the people who must act on them.

This is a sector we know from the inside.
