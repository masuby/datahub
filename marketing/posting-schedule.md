## Rules that keep this alive

- **Never miss two days in a row.** One missed day is nothing; two is how campaigns die.
- Sunday is a rest day. Take it — this has to survive past October.
- If you have no new poster for a Status day, **repost the best-performing earlier one**. Repetition is not a failure; most of your audience missed it the first time.
- Post Status in the **evening (19:00–21:00)** — that is when people in Dar are on their phones.
- Post to the feed in the **morning (07:00–09:00)**, on a weekday.
- After each post, add `— posted YYYY-MM-DD` to the bottom of that folder's `caption.md`.
