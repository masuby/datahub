# DataHub brand sheet

Every poster must look like it came from the same company as the website. These
are the exact values used on datahub.co.tz — copy them into Canva, Figma or
whatever you make the posters in.

---

## Colours

| Role | Hex | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | `#0a0f1d` | The poster background. Always dark. |
| Surface | `#0f1626` | Cards and panels sitting on the background |
| Foreground | `#e7edf6` | Main text |
| Muted | `#94a3b8` | Secondary text |
| **Accent (cyan)** | `#22d3ee` | The main highlight colour |
| **Accent 2 (emerald)** | `#34d399` | The second highlight colour |
| Border | `#1e293b` | Thin dividing lines |

**The signature gradient** — used on the logo mark, buttons and the site
address — is a 135° linear gradient from `#22d3ee` to `#34d399`.

Never put DataHub content on a white background. The dark canvas is the brand.

---

## Type

The site uses **Geist**. If you cannot get it, use **Inter** — it is nearly
identical and free everywhere. Failing that, Poppins or system sans.

| Element | Weight | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headline | Bold / 600–700 | Tight letter spacing, generous line height |
| Body | Regular / 400 | Never below 32px on a 1080-wide poster |
| Site address | Bold | In the accent gradient or `#34d399` |

---

## The logo mark

A rounded square (about 16px radius at 60px size) filled with the cyan→emerald
gradient, containing a bold letter **D** in the background colour `#0a0f1d`.
Beside it: **Data** in `#e7edf6` and **Hub** in `#22d3ee`.

---

## Non-negotiables for every poster

1. **The address `www.datahub.co.tz` appears on every single poster**, at the
   bottom, in the accent colour, large enough to read at arm's length.
2. Dark background. Always.
3. One idea per poster. If it needs a paragraph, it belongs in the caption, not
   on the image.
4. For Stories and Status (1080 × 1920), keep everything important out of the
   **top 250px and bottom 250px** — the WhatsApp and Instagram interface covers
   those areas.
5. Never use a stock photo of a smiling person pointing at a chart. Use the real
   dashboard, real type, or a clean icon.
